How it works

Four steps. You only do the first.

01

Take a screenshot

Use the shortcuts you already know. Full screen, selection or clipboard, ShotCubby catches them all.

02

It files itself

The capture lands in your ShotCubby folder, in a new folder for each day, before it can clutter your Desktop.

03

AI names it

With AI naming on, a downscaled copy goes to your Claude or OpenAI key and the file is renamed after what it shows. Off, you rename it yourself in one click.

04

Find it in a click

Press ⌘Ctrl / to search by the new name, or click the menu bartray icon to browse, copy or reveal any capture.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is ShotCubby really free?

Yes. No account, no subscription, no paid tier. AI naming needs your own API key from Claude (Anthropic) or OpenAI. It is not a Claude or ChatGPT subscription: you create the key in your developer account and add a few dollars of credit, and your provider bills by usage. A screenshot costs a fraction of a cent.

How does AI naming work?

Turn it on in Settings, paste your Claude (Anthropic) or OpenAI API key, and ShotCubby sends a downscaled copy of each new screenshot to that provider to get a short, descriptive file name. The file on disk is renamed. If the request fails, the name stays as it is.

Which AI providers are supported?

Anthropic (Claude) and OpenAI. You bring your own API key, so you stay in control of the account, the usage and the cost.

Can I choose how my screenshots are named?

Yes. Add naming instructions in Settings, for example “lowercase kebab-case, start with the app name, in English”. The AI follows your convention over its defaults.

Is AI naming private?

It is off by default. When it is on, only a downscaled copy of each new screenshot goes to the provider you chose, with your key. Nothing passes through a ShotCubby server, there is none. Your key is stored in the macOS KeychainWindows Credential Manager.

Where are my screenshots stored?

In a ShotCubby folder, organized in one new folder per day. Files stay on your computer, as plain images you can open anywhere.

Does it collect any data?

No telemetry and no account. Without AI naming, ShotCubby works offline and your captures never leave your machine.

Which systems are supported?

macOS 14.0 or later and Windows. The macOS build is signed and notarized by Apple.

How do I find an old screenshot?

Press ⌘Ctrl / to search by name, or click the menu bartray icon to see all your captures. The shortcut can be changed in Settings.

What happens when I rename a screenshot?

The file on disk is renamed too, so Finder and SpotlightFile Explorer and Windows Search find it under its new name.

Are clipboard screenshots saved?

Yes. Captures copied to the clipboard are saved as files, so the next copy does not erase them.
